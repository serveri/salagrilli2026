What is this?

This is a small CTF, "Capture The Flag". Each task is a puzzle that hides a secret piece of text called a flag. Solve the puzzle, find the flag, and you unlock a reward. Collect the rewards from all the tasks and you'll learn where and when Salagrillit happens.

What does a flag look like?

Every flag has the same shape:

SALA{s0m3_t3xt}

When a task is solved you'll spot something in that format, in a picture, a sound, some code, a game, or hidden in the page itself. That whole thing, curly braces and all, is what you submit.

How do I play?

  1. On the main page, click a numbered circle to open a task.
  2. Hit Open task and work out the puzzle.
  3. Find the flag, type it into the Enter the flag box, and Submit.
  4. Right flag → the circle turns green and your reward appears.

Stuck?

See the chat bubble in the bottom-right corner? That's a hint bot. Ask it for a nudge on any task, it gives hints, not answers, so you still get to solve it yourself. Please don't burn all of the sausage and Jaloviina budget on AI tokens.
